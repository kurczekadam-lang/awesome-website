The American Red Cross is a nonprofit organisation that provides relief assistance worldwide. The American Red Cross has supported people during notable disasters such as the sinking of the Titanic, the Spanish Flu, Hurricane Katrina, and both World Wars. The American Red Cross has helped hundreds, if not millions, of people around the world, making it an honest cause to support.
 

History


Source: wikipedia

Clara Barton was a pioneer in nursing and humanitarian work; she established the Red Cross in New York in 1881 after learning about the Red Cross in Geneva, Switzerland. Clara was destined to bring the organization to America.
 
Clara Barton was the Red Cross President for 23 years; in 1904 she retired. To this day, Clara's legacy lives on with the American Red Cross's spirit.

Disaster Releif

When a huricane, flood, or wildfire hits, the Red Cross provides food, and shelter to the communities. The Red Cross works directly with the goverment to coordinate effective support.

During large disaster, the Red Cross sets up shelters, meals, and sends mental health workers to deal with tramatic experinces. 

Blood Donation

The Red Cross provides about 40% of the nation's blood supply. That blood supports hospitals, cancer treatments, and chronic conditions. To keep up with the demand for blood, the Red Cross needs consistent donations.

The organization runs blood drives in almost every community; you have probably seen their blood buses at your local church. The free Blood Donor app lets you find nearby drives, schedule appointments, and get free pre-donation screening. Donors get special perks and offers as well, like gift cards, discounts, and a free cookie!

Certificaiton

CPR, AED, first aid, life support, babysitting, and lifeguarding are all certifications that the Red Cross provides. Courses are available in person, online, or as blended learning. Many businesses require a Red Cross certification to operate in the workplace. Many of the certifications are OSHA-compliant, which is why employers require them for workplace safety. Certifications are usually renewed every one to two years.

Check out Red Cross Courses and lcheck out the classes near you.

Military Family Support

Hero Care Network is a service provided by the American Red Cross. It focuses on free, confidential assistance for service members and their families. When a military family experinces an emergecy they call the help line to verify the information and notify the military commands of the message.

The Red Cross also connects with eligible military families with finacial aid for travel, food, and shelter assistance. They work with military aid organizations like the VA to determine eligibility. For military families, they provide programs for children that teach communication and coping skills.

International Humanitrain Aid

The American Red Cross is a part of the largest humanitarian network in the world. The Internation Red Cross helps communities in other countries to prepare, respond, and recover from disasters. The biggest focus is childhood vaccination. Since 2001, the Red Cross has helped vaccinate over 2 billion children. The Red Cross also helps train local responders, improve clean water, and sanitation.
